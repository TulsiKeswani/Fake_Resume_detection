Step5:- AI Work

* AI Will Take Uploaded Resumes of the candidates  
* AI Parse the Resume   
* AI Will Check the confidence score of resume that How much possibility that resume is generated by AI or not  
* After that AI Will Verify Users Skills by follow the given Steps:-  
  * Visiting User Github Profile and check for their projects the stack used, commits history, Abstract Syntax Tree, Projects complexity etc  
  * Model Will fetch data of user available online like leetcode questions count linkdien profile etc  
  * Finally After deep analysis ai will make calculate a score and store for final process

Step6:- AI Interview Process:-

* AI Will automatically starts an interview of the candidate as candidate apply for the job  
* The Interview will be completely based on Resume Provided by the candidate  
* The interview will be completely protected all the suspicious activity will be detected, majored, and stored for future reports  
* The interview will not a normal question and answering interview this a complete simulation of real interview In which:  
  * AI Asks Questions from candidates  
  * AI generates cross questioning on candidates answers  
  * AI Will Tweak the questions by asking wrong questions and fake details so that he can check the candidates knowledge is actual or ai provided only  
  * Code editor will be provided for technical interviews where ai asks coding questions, wrong statements write code type questions   
  * Also simultaneously AI Will Monitor aspects like confidence,clarity,professionalism,communication,fluency etc  
  * Final Interview report generate for the Candidate and scores of different aspects will be store for company dashboard job report
